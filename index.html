Questions

Which case should I get?

Bringing your whole collection: the 5-Row Trolley, up to 250 slabs. Flying to shows: the 4-Row Trolley, carry-on size for most major airlines. Want your four best on the table: the 4-Row Display. Want a small case for your top cards: the 2-Row Display.

How does ordering ahead work?

On each case you want, pick the show you're coming to and your slab mags, then hit "Add to cart." Check out once and pay for everything together. Your order is set aside with your name on it. Come to our table, show your ID and it's yours.

Why pay now instead of at the table?

Cases at the show are limited. A paid order is set aside with your name on it before the doors open. Walk-up cases sell until they're gone.

What do I need to pick it up?

Your ID. Give us the name on the order and it's yours.

What's a slab mag?

A magnetic case that holds one graded slab. Show specials come with two free with every case, PSA or BGS size, your pick.

Will my slabs fit?

The trolley cases fit PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC and TAG slabs. The display cases fit PSA, BGS, CGC and TAG.

Can I carry a trolley on a plane?

The 4-Row Trolley is built to meet carry-on size limits for most major airlines. Check with your airline before you fly. The 5-Row may be too big to carry on.

What if I order and can't make the show?

Text us before the show and we'll hold it for the next show you're at or ship it to you.

Can I just buy one at the table?

Yes, while they last. Cases are limited, so order ahead and yours is held.
